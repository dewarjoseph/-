\begin{abstract}
We isolate and independently verify the numerical continuum limits of the 3-wave Bragg bispectrum pseudoscalar $\chi_b(\infty)$ and the dynamical graph-Laplacian circular dichroism $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Lap}}(\infty)$ on the 6D $\to$ 3D chiral icosahedral cut-and-project quasicrystal. First, we provide the complete orthonormal $3\times 6$ projection matrices $P_\parallel$ and $P_\perp$ over $\mathbb{Q}(\tau)$ ($\tau = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$), explicitly documenting the reconciled $+\tau$ entry at $(V_\perp)_{2,4}$ that restores all four projector orthogonality and completeness identities to $< 2.3\times 10^{-16}$ in IEEE-754 double precision. Second, we specify the exact 60-vertex chiral acceptance polytope $W_b \subset E_\perp$ generated by the proper icosahedral orbit $I$ of the seed $\mathbf{p}_0 = (0.08, 0.25, 1.48)^\top$ ($\mathrm{Vol}(W_b) = 11.79855893$). Third, we evaluate the Kronecker--Weyl tetrahedral integrals directly from scratch across Gauss--Legendre quadrature orders $N_{\mathrm{GL}} \in \{8, 12, 16, 18\}$, demonstrating convergence to $\chi_b(\infty) = 0.00176684$, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Lap}}(\infty) = 0.00153940$, and the stable universal ratio $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Lap}}(\infty)/\chi_b(\infty) = 0.8712716\dots \to 0.8713$. Fourth, we audit the raw floating-point parity cancellations on the mirror enantiomorph $-W_b$ ($\max |B_\infty(W_b)+B_\infty(-W_b)| = 4.09\times 10^{-15}$) and on the achiral rhombic triacontahedron $W_a$ ($\chi_b(W_a) = 3.02\times 10^{-15}$, $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Lap}}(W_a) = 2.94\times 10^{-15}$ at $N_{\mathrm{GL}}=16$).
\end{abstract}

\section{Canonical $6\mathrm{D} \to 3\mathrm{D}$ Projectors and Reconciled $+\tau$ Entry}

Let $\tau = \frac{1+\sqrt{5}}{2}$ and $\mathcal{N} = \sqrt{2(1+\tau^2)} = \sqrt{4+2\tau} \approx 2.6900635$. The physical ($P_\parallel = \mathcal{N}^{-1} V_\parallel$) and internal perpendicular ($P_\perp = \mathcal{N}^{-1} V_\perp$) $3\times 6$ projection matrices are:
\begin{equation}
V_\parallel = \begin{pmatrix}
1 & -1 & 0 & 0 & \tau & \tau \\
\tau & \tau & 1 & -1 & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & \tau & \tau & 1 & -1
\end{pmatrix}, \qquad
V_\perp = \begin{pmatrix}
-\tau & \tau & 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\
1 & 1 & -\tau & \mathbf{+\tau} & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 1 & 1 & -\tau & \tau
\end{pmatrix}.
\end{equation}
With the reconciled $+\tau$ entry at Row~2, Column~4 of $V_\perp$, direct IEEE-754 evaluation (\texttt{src/icosahedral\_qc/projectors.py}) confirms:
\begin{equation}
\|P_\parallel P_\parallel^\top - I_3\|_\infty = 0.0, \quad \|P_\perp P_\perp^\top - I_3\|_\infty = 0.0, \quad \|P_\parallel P_\perp^\top\|_\infty = 1.69\times 10^{-16}, \quad \|P_\parallel^\top P_\parallel + P_\perp^\top P_\perp - I_6\|_\infty = 2.22\times 10^{-16}.
\end{equation}

\section{Chiral Acceptance Window $W_b$ and Kronecker--Weyl Continuum Integrals}

Let $I \subset \mathrm{SO}(3)$ denote the 60-element proper icosahedral rotation group acting in $E_\perp$, and let $\mathbf{p}_0 = (0.08, 0.25, 1.48)^\top \in E_\perp$. The chiral acceptance window is the 60-vertex convex hull $W_b = \mathrm{conv}\{R\mathbf{p}_0 : R \in I\}$ with exact volume $\mathrm{Vol}(W_b) = 11.7985589273$. Its mirror enantiomorph is $-W_b = \mathrm{conv}\{-R\mathbf{p}_0 : R \in I\}$, and the achiral control window is the 32-vertex rhombic triacontahedron $W_a = P_\perp([-0.5,0.5]^6)$ ($\mathrm{Vol}(W_a) = 12.94427191$).

By Kronecker--Weyl equidistribution of $x_\perp(\mathbf{n}) = P_\perp \mathbf{n}$ inside $W \subset E_\perp$ as patch radius $R_{\max} \to \infty$, the structure factor and bond-shifted overlap integrals converge to:
\begin{equation}
F_\infty(\mathbf{k}_m) = \frac{1}{\mathrm{Vol}(W)} \int_W e^{-i \mathbf{k}_{\perp,m}\cdot \mathbf{y}} \, d^3\mathbf{y}, \qquad
F_{\alpha,\infty}^*(\mathbf{k}_m) = \frac{1}{\mathrm{Vol}(W)} \int_{W \cap (W - \mathbf{d}_{\perp,\alpha})} e^{+i \mathbf{k}_{\perp,m}\cdot \mathbf{y}} \, d^3\mathbf{y},
\end{equation}
where $\mathbf{d}_{\perp,\alpha} = P_\perp \mathbf{e}_\alpha$ ($\alpha \in \{0,\dots,5\}$) and each intersection polytope $W \cap (W - \mathbf{d}_{\perp,\alpha})$ is computed exactly via halfspace intersection (\texttt{scipy.spatial.HalfspaceIntersection}) and star-triangulated into tetrahedra from its centroid.

\section{Independent Analytic-Sinc 2D Triangle Reduction Cross-Check}

To verify the 3D Gauss--Legendre tetrahedral quadrature via an independent semi-analytical integration scheme, we integrate the inner barycentric coordinate $b_3 \in [0, 1 - b_1 - b_2]$ analytically in closed form on every tetrahedron $T_s \subset W$:
\begin{equation}
\int_0^{h(b_1,b_2)} e^{i(\alpha_1 b_1 + \alpha_2 b_2 + \alpha_3 b_3)}\, db_3 = h(b_1,b_2)\, e^{i\left(\alpha_1 b_1 + \alpha_2 b_2 + \frac{1}{2}\alpha_3 h(b_1,b_2)\right)} \operatorname{sinc}\!\left(\frac{\alpha_3 h(b_1,b_2)}{2\pi}\right),
\end{equation}
where $h(b_1,b_2) = 1 - b_1 - b_2$ and $\operatorname{sinc}(x/\pi) = \frac{\sin x}{x}$ is entire and free of small-denominator singularities when $\alpha_3 \to 0$. This reduces the 3D tetrahedral integral to a 2D triangle quadrature requiring only $N_{2\mathrm{D}}^2 = 400$ nodes per simplex at $N_{2\mathrm{D}} = 20$ (compared to $N_{\mathrm{GL}}^3 = 4{,}096$ nodes at $N_{\mathrm{GL}} = 16$, a $10.24\times$ node reduction). Evaluating $\chi_b(\infty)$ and $\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Lap}}(\infty)$ via this Analytic-Sinc 2D Triangle Reduction (\texttt{evaluate\_analytic\_sinc\_2d\_continuum\_cross\_check}) yields:
\begin{equation}
\chi_b^{\text{2D-sinc}}(\infty) = 0.001766843873, \qquad \mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Lap}}^{\text{2D-sinc}}(\infty) = 0.001539400922, \qquad \frac{\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Lap}}^{\text{2D-sinc}}(\infty)}{\chi_b^{\text{2D-sinc}}(\infty)} = 0.8712716192 \to \mathbf{0.8713},
\end{equation}
agreeing with the 3D 16-point Gauss--Legendre evaluation to $|\Delta\chi_b| = 1.68\times 10^{-12}$, $|\Delta\mathcal{D}_{\mathrm{Lap}}| = 1.14\times 10^{-12}$, and $|\Delta\mathcal{R}_{\mathrm{Weyl}}| = 1.82\times 10^{-10}$.
