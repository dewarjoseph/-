# The Chair44 Clifford $Q_8$ Connection: End-to-End Derivation and Audit

Scope. This document takes the face-normal Clifford connection
$C^{(f)}_{ab}=(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_a)(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_b)\in Q_8\subset SU(2)$
exactly as it is defined in this repository (`src/chair44/clifford.py`, `src/chair44/fr30_frames.py`,
`certificates/Chair44Certificates.lean`, `certificates/PvsNPCliffordCertificates.lean`,
`deliverables/chair44_clifford_holonomy_pure_math.tex`, `paper/main.tex` Sections 1.3 and 2) and follows it as far
as the mathematics goes: what the construction is, what gauge structure it determines, whether a continuum gauge
theory or the Standard Model can be obtained from it, what dynamics it supports, what is proved, and what is
false or unsupported. Every numerical statement below was recomputed from the definitions with code that does not
import the repository (`audit/independent_clifford_q8_audit.py`, `audit/controls_plaquettes_spin4_solder_audit.py`),
and both Lean files were re-checked with the Lean 4.34.1 release binary.

## 0. Verdict

1. **What is true and proved.** The FR30 substitution produces an exact, overlap-free, gap-free chair supertile at
   every level tested (1 to 4). The eight Level-1 frames lie in $SO(3,\mathbb Z)$. The Level-1 contact Laplacian
   and its characteristic polynomial $\lambda(\lambda-24)(\lambda^2-30\lambda+207)(\lambda^2-21\lambda+99)^2$ are
   correct. The Clifford links lie in $Q_8$, the Wilson loops are gauge covariant, and the holonomy group is all of
   $Q_8$ at levels 1, 2 and 3. Both Lean files compile with zero errors, zero `sorry`, and only the standard axioms
   `propext`, `Quot.sound`, `Classical.choice`.

2. **What the construction is.** It is a single, frozen, finite-group lattice gauge configuration. In the
   "ambient" gauge it is the spinor coboundary of the frames conjugated by the face-normal Pauli matrix,
   $U_aC_{ab}U_b^\dagger=N_f\,(U_aU_b^\dagger)\,N_f$, so its entire non-triviality comes from the fact that the
   conjugating Pauli matrix differs from edge to edge (Theorem 1.2 below). It is determined by the frame
   *decorations* of the chairs, not by the point set of the tiling (Theorem 1.3 and the explicit counterexample in
   Section 1.4). It has no dynamical degrees of freedom and no coupling constant.

3. **Continuum limit.** None exists as a Lie-algebra-valued connection. Every link is at geodesic distance
   $0,\ \pi/2$ or $\pi$ from the identity in $SU(2)$ at every level, so no mesh-refinement expansion
   $C=\exp(aA)$ is possible (Proposition 2.3). The only continuum avatar of a $Q_8$-valued configuration is a
   topological (Dijkgraaf–Witten type) theory with no propagating gauge bosons. A Lie limit would exist only under
   an additional smoothness hypothesis on the frame field that the actual $SO(3,\mathbb Z)$ frames violate; under
   that hypothesis the limit is derived in Proposition 2.4 and turns out to be a functional of the frame field
   (the spin connection projected transverse to the face normal), not an independent gauge field.

4. **Standard Model.** Not derivable, and the repository nowhere claims it (there is no occurrence of SU(3),
   hypercharge, Yang–Mills, Higgs or a coupling constant in any source, paper or certificate). The obstruction is
   exact: the holonomy group is finite, so the Lie algebra it generates is zero while $\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus
   \mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)$ has dimension 12; every link has $\det C_{ab}=1$, so there is no abelian
   flux at all and nothing that could be a hypercharge; there are no fermion fields, no Lorentzian signature, no
   chirality operator, no triplication of anything. Section 3 states the minimal structure one would have to add,
   and it is the whole Standard Model.

5. **Claims that are false or unsupported in the repository.**
   - The monograph's holonomy table (Table `tab:q8_holonomy`: "48 cycles, 100% non-trivial, 81.2% traceless" at
     level 1; 416 cycles at level 2; 3424 at level 3) is irreproducible. The Level-$k$ panel multigraph has
     $|E|-|V|+1 = 41,\ 513,\ 4865$ fundamental cycles, of which $58.5\%,\ 69.2\%,\ 67.7\%$ are non-trivial. The
     pure-math deliverable and the data package carry the correct level-1 numbers (24/41), so the repository
     contradicts itself.
   - The "resolution of Kamenetsky's Open Problem 9.1" rests on a $64\times 6$ matrix that is generated by an
     arbitrary index formula (`idx*7+3 mod 64`, with $\pm1$ filler) in
     `src/chair44/machine_checked_certificates.py:certify_4d_c4_obstruction_rank_exact`. No frame assignment
     $\pi:U_4\to S_4$ enters. Other arbitrary pairings also give rank 6 with different Gram determinants
     (Section 5.3). The Lean certificate proves only the linear algebra of the stored matrix. The open problem is
     not addressed.
   - The "soldered bundle mass gap" ($\operatorname{rank}=24$, all translational zero modes lifted) is a
     gauge-inconsistency artifact: the operator transports one coupling with the identity and the other with the
     frame coboundary $M_a^\top M_b$. Transporting both with the same connection restores the three pure-gauge
     zero modes (rank 21). The stored $24\times24$ matrix also has $\lambda_{\min}=2.9089$, not the published
     $2.6687$.
   - $|P^*|=44$, $|F^*|=135$ and "coarsening closed" are hardcoded constants in `src/chair44/frames.py` and
     `machine_checked_certificates.py`, not computed. Only $|P_0|=30$ is computed.
   - The discrete connection one-form $\mathcal A_{ab}=\log C_{ab}$ of `paper/main.tex` Section 2.3 is undefined
     on half of the Level-1 links: 24 of the 48 links equal $-I_2$, whose logarithm in $\mathfrak{su}(2)$ is not
     unique.
   - The cosmology pillar's "Chair44 inflation eigenvalue $\lambda_{PF}=\tau^3$" is wrong for the chair
     substitution (its Perron–Frobenius eigenvalue is $8$, as the repository's own Pillar 6 states), and the CMB
     birefringence angle $\beta_0=0.35^\circ$ is an input parameter, not a derived quantity.

6. **What can be predicted.** Nothing about particle physics. Two sharp, model-level, falsifiable predictions do
   follow (Section 6): the exact spectrum of the Level-1 Clifford hopping operator, and the limiting frequencies of
   gauge-invariant plaquette curvature in the infinite FR30 tiling (computed to level 5: non-trivial plaquette
   fraction $56.7\%\to51.4\%\to49.0\%\to48.1\%\to47.6\%$).

## 1. Discrete foundations

### 1.1 Axioms as implemented

**A1 (Prototile).** $C_3=[0,2]^3\setminus(1,2]^3$, the union of the seven unit cubes with corners in
$U_3=\{0,1\}^3\setminus\{(1,1,1)\}$. $C_3$ is invariant under the coordinate-permutation group $S_3$.

**A2 (Substitution, doubled coordinates).** A pose is $(G,\mathbf T)\in SO(3,\mathbb Z)\ltimes\mathbb Z^3$. One
inflation step maps a pose to eight child poses
$$(G,\mathbf T)\ \mapsto\ (G\pi_0,\ 2\mathbf T+G(1,1,1)^\top),\qquad (G\,D_s(\mathbf v)\pi_{\mathbf v},\ 2\mathbf T+4G\mathbf v)\quad(\mathbf v\in U_3),$$
with $D_s(\mathbf v)=\operatorname{diag}(1-2v_1,1-2v_2,1-2v_3)$ and $\pi_{\mathbf v}$ a permutation matrix.
Determinant bookkeeping: $\det D_s(\mathbf v)=(-1)^{|\mathbf v|}$, so $\det(D_s\pi_{\mathbf v})=+1$ forces
$\operatorname{sgn}\pi_{\mathbf v}=(-1)^{|\mathbf v|}$. The level-$k$ supertile is the set of $8^k$ tiles
obtained by iterating from $(I_3,\mathbf 0)$.

**A3 (FR30 frames).** $\pi_0=\pi_{(000)}=\pi_{(101)}=\mathrm{id}$, $\pi_{(001)}=(0\,2\,1)$,
$\pi_{(010)}=(2\,1\,0)$, $\pi_{(011)}=(1\,2\,0)$, $\pi_{(100)}=(1\,0\,2)$, $\pi_{(110)}=(2\,0\,1)$
(`src/chair44/fr30_frames.py`). This is attributed to Kamenetsky (arXiv:2610.00916); the repository does not
contain a derivation of it or a check of its aperiodicity or of the closure numbers $|P^*|=44$, $|F^*|=135$
(those two numbers are literals in the code).

**A4 (Panel multigraph).** Vertices are tiles. For every pair of unit cubes $c\in a$, $c'\in b$ with $a\neq b$
and $c'=c+\mathbf e_\mu$ there is one directed edge $(a,b)$ with ambient normal $\mathbf n_f=\mathbf e_\mu$ (and
its reverse with $-\mathbf e_\mu$). At level 1: $|V|=8$, $|E|=48$.

**A5 (Clifford link).** With $\mathbf u_a^{(f)}=M_a^\top\mathbf n_f$, $\mathbf u_b^{(f)}=M_b^\top\mathbf n_f$,
$$C^{(f)}_{ab}=(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_a^{(f)})(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_b^{(f)})
=(\mathbf u_a\cdot\mathbf u_b)\,I_2+i\,(\mathbf u_a\times\mathbf u_b)\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma .$$
Since $\mathbf u_a,\mathbf u_b$ are signed coordinate axes, $C_{ab}\in Q_8=\{\pm I,\pm i\sigma_1,\pm i\sigma_2,\pm i\sigma_3\}$,
and $C^{(f)}_{ba}=C^{(f)\dagger}_{ab}$ (reversing $\mathbf n_f$ reverses the product).

**A6 (Wilson loop).** For a closed edge path $\gamma=(a_0,a_1,\dots,a_m=a_0)$,
$W(\gamma)=C_{a_0a_1}C_{a_1a_2}\cdots C_{a_{m-1}a_0}$. Gauge transformations are
$C_{ab}\mapsto G_aC_{ab}G_b^{-1}$ with $G_a\in SU(2)$ (or $Q_8$).

### 1.3 Structure theorems (new; proved here, checked numerically)

Let $U_a\in SU(2)$ be a spin lift of $M_a$, i.e. $U_a(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf v)U_a^\dagger=\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot(M_a\mathbf v)$,
and write $N_f=\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf n_f$ (so $N_f^2=I$).

**Theorem 1.1 (factorization).** $\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_a^{(f)}=U_a^\dagger N_fU_a$, hence
$$C^{(f)}_{ab}=U_a^\dagger N_f\,U_aU_b^\dagger\,N_f\,U_b .$$
*Proof.* $\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot(M_a^\top\mathbf n)=U_a^\dagger(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf n)U_a$ by the
defining property of the lift applied to $M_a^{-1}=M_a^\top$. Multiply the two factors. $\square$

**Theorem 1.2 (ambient gauge: a face-normal-twisted coboundary).** In the gauge $G_a=U_a$,
$$C'_{ab}=U_aC_{ab}U_b^\dagger=N_f\,\hat V_{ab}\,N_f^{-1},\qquad \hat V_{ab}=U_aU_b^\dagger .$$
$\hat V$ is the spinor lift of the relative-frame coboundary $M_aM_b^\top$, whose loops telescope to $\pm I$.
The Clifford connection is this coboundary conjugated, edge by edge, by the $\pi$-rotation $N_f$ about the face
normal. Along a loop,
$$W(\gamma)\ \sim\ \prod_{k}\ U_{a_{k}}^\dagger\,\bigl(N_{f_{k-1}}N_{f_k}\bigr)\,U_{a_k},$$
the product over the tiles visited of the *turning rotor* between the incoming and outgoing face normals written in
that tile's local frame. The frame-free product $\prod_k N_{f_{k-1}}N_{f_k}$ telescopes to $I$ because $N^2=I$;
the non-triviality is exactly the failure of the conjugations $U_{a_k}^\dagger(\cdot)U_{a_k}$ to cancel when the
frames differ from tile to tile. *Proof.* Insert Theorem 1.1 into the product and regroup; verified for all 48
links numerically. $\square$

**Theorem 1.3 (frame changes are not gauge transformations).** Replacing $M_a\mapsto M_aR_a$ at a single tile
($R_a\in SO(3)$) changes $C_{ab}\mapsto U^{R\dagger}_a(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_a)U^R_a(\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_b)$,
which is not of the form $G_aC_{ab}G_b^{-1}$ unless $U^R_a$ commutes with $\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_a$ for
every face of $a$. Consequently the holonomy data are not functions of the tiling as a point set; they are
functions of the frame decoration.

### 1.4 Counterexample: the connection depends on the decoration, not on the tiling

$C_3$ is $S_3$-invariant, so replacing $\pi_{\mathbf v}$ by any permutation of the same parity leaves every tile,
cube and panel of the tiling unchanged. Replacing the frame of tile 3 at level 1 by $M_3\cdot(1\,2\,0)$ (an even
permutation) changes the fundamental-cycle trace multiset from $\{-2:1,\ 0:23,\ 2:17\}$ to
$\{-2:1,\ 0:22,\ 2:18\}$. Two identical tilings therefore carry different $Q_8$ connections. "Parameter-free"
holds only relative to the FR30 decoration being fixed by fiat.

## 2. Gauge structure

### 2.1 Holonomy group and gauge-invariant content

The link values generate $Q_8$. Taking a spanning tree and all fundamental cycles, the generated holonomy group is
the full $Q_8$ at levels 1, 2 and 3 (computed; the deliverable states this only for level 1). The complete
gauge-invariant content of a connection on a graph is the conjugacy class of the holonomy representation
$\rho:\pi_1(\Gamma_k)\to Q_8$; with $b_1=41,513,4865$ free generators this is a finite set. Nothing continuous is
present. The per-cycle distribution quoted in the pure-math deliverable, Counts $=\{+1:17,-1:1,+i:6,\dots\}$, is
spanning-tree dependent (the repository's own adversarial test observes non-trivial fractions varying with the
tree); it is not an invariant.

On vector (spin-1) matter the connection acts through $Q_8\to SO(3)$, whose image is the Klein group
$\{I,R_x(\pi),R_y(\pi),R_z(\pi)\}\cong\mathbb Z_2\times\mathbb Z_2$, which is abelian. The connection is
non-abelian only on spin-1/2 matter. Its central part ($W=-I$) is a $\mathbb Z_2$ sign flux; it appears on 1 of 41
fundamental cycles at level 1 and on $5\%$ to $8\%$ of inter-tile plaquettes (Section 6.2).

**Proposition 2.1 (not a coboundary, in any gauge).** If some cycle has $W(\gamma)\neq I$, no vertex assignment
$\sigma$ gives $C=\sigma_a^{-1}\sigma_b$. (Lean: `universal_nontrivial_holonomy_implies_unsat`, proved for all groups
by induction.) Since cycles with $\operatorname{Tr}W=0$ exist, the Clifford connection is not pure gauge. This is the
precise content of "escaping the coboundary trap".

**Proposition 2.2 (what gauge invariance proves).** $W\mapsto G_{a_0}WG_{a_0}^{-1}$ under any $G$, so
$\operatorname{Tr}W$ and the conjugacy class are invariant (Lean: `universal_gauge_transformed_path`). The
"$512/512$ no-go" is the specialization to $G_a\in Q_8$ on one 3-cycle and adds nothing beyond this.

### 2.2 Continuum limit

**Proposition 2.3 (no Lie-algebra-valued limit).** For every level $k$ and every link,
$d_{SU(2)}(C_{ab},I)\in\{0,\pi/2,\pi\}$. A continuum limit with $C_e=\exp(a_kA_e)$, $a_k\to0$, $A_e$ bounded,
requires $d(C_e,I)\to0$. Hence no such limit exists along the substitution hierarchy. The substitution does not
renormalize the connection: level-$k$ links are computed from frames by the same rule, with the same three
possible values.

What remains is a finite-group gauge configuration. Its continuum description, if any, is a topological field
theory with gauge group $Q_8$ (Dijkgraaf–Witten type), whose Hilbert space on any closed surface is finite
dimensional and which has no local propagating excitations, no field strength in $\mathfrak{su}(2)$, no coupling
constant and no field equations. The identification $\mathcal A=\log C$ in `paper/main.tex` eq. for Section 2.3 is
not even well defined on the 24 Level-1 links equal to $-I$.

**Proposition 2.4 (the only route to a Lie limit, and what it gives).** Suppose, contrary to A3, that frames
varied smoothly so that adjacent relative rotations were $V_{ab}=M_a^\top M_b=\exp(\epsilon\,\omega_{ab}\!\times)$
with $\epsilon\to0$. Using $\mathbf u_a=V_{ab}\mathbf u_b$ and $N_b=\boldsymbol\sigma\cdot\mathbf u_b$,
$$C_{ab}=\hat V_{ab}N_b\hat V_{ab}^{\dagger}N_b=I+\epsilon\,(X-N_bXN_b)+O(\epsilon^2),\qquad X=\tfrac{i}{2}\,\omega_{ab}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma,$$
so $A_{ab}=i\,(\omega_{ab})_{\perp}\cdot\boldsymbol\sigma$ with $(\omega)_\perp$ the component of the frame's
rotation vector perpendicular to the face normal. The limiting connection is the spin connection of the frame field
projected transverse to the face normal: a functional of the frame field with no independent degrees of freedom.
In the actual tiling the hypothesis fails ($\epsilon$ is $\pi/2$ or $\pi$), so even this does not apply.

## 3. Standard Model reconstruction

The repository does not claim it. Attempting it from the construction fails at every required step, for reasons that
are exact rather than matters of technique.

| Required ingredient | What the construction provides | Obstruction |
|---|---|---|
| Gauge Lie algebra $\mathfrak{su}(3)\oplus\mathfrak{su}(2)\oplus\mathfrak u(1)$, dim 12 | holonomy group $Q_8$, finite | the Lie algebra generated by the holonomy is $0$ |
| $U(1)_Y$ flux / hypercharge | $\det C_{ab}=1$ on every link | no abelian holonomy exists anywhere; no quantity to call a charge |
| $SU(3)_C$, complex triplets | frames act on $\mathbb R^3$ through the octahedral group $O\cong S_4\subset SO(3,\mathbb Z)$ | real, finite, 24 elements; no complex 3-dimensional structure |
| Chiral (Weyl) fermions | a two-component hopping index with $T^2=-1$ | Euclidean 3D; no Lorentz signature, no $\gamma_5$, no Dirac operator |
| Three generations | an 8-fold substitution hierarchy | a hierarchy of scales, not a triplication of identical representations |
| Anomaly cancellation, Yukawas, Higgs | nothing | no matter content to constrain |

The *minimal additional structure* that would be needed is: a principal $G$-bundle with
$G\supseteq SU(3)\times SU(2)\times U(1)$ and *dynamical* link variables (the $Q_8$ configuration could at most be
a frozen background flux inside the $SU(2)$ factor); a Wilson-type action with a coupling; Grassmann fields in
chosen representations with a 4D Lorentzian (or reflection-positive Euclidean) signature and a chiral projection;
and a Higgs/Yukawa sector. None of this is supplied or constrained by the tiling, which would only furnish the
lattice. The claim that a discrete $SU(2)$-valued connection "yields" the Standard Model is therefore false for
this construction, and the result is decisive rather than open.

## 4. Dynamics

**What the repository actually defines.** A single-particle hopping operator on the tile graph,
$$H=\sum_{(a,b,f)}\bigl(|a\rangle\langle a|+|b\rangle\langle b|\bigr)\otimes I_2-\sum_{(a,b,f)}\bigl(|a\rangle\langle b|\otimes C^{(f)}_{ab}+\text{h.c.}\bigr),$$
(`build_clifford_hamiltonian`). It is Hermitian, positive semidefinite, commutes with $T=\mathbb 1\otimes i\sigma_yK$
with $T^2=-1$ (so every level is Kramers-degenerate; verified), and its Level-1 characteristic polynomial is exactly
$$P(\lambda)^2,\quad P=\lambda^8-96\lambda^7+3870\lambda^6-85254\lambda^5+1117638\lambda^4-8881920\lambda^3+41520357\lambda^2-103483494\lambda+103796478 .$$

**What does not exist.** (i) An action for the gauge field: the links are fixed by geometry, so a Wilson action
$S=\beta\sum_p(1-\tfrac12\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Tr}W_p)$ evaluates to a constant (at level 4,
$0.56\,\beta$ per inter-tile plaquette from the curvature frequencies of Section 6.2). (ii) Field equations for the
connection: there are no gauge degrees of freedom to vary. (iii) Interaction terms: $H$ is quadratic. (iv) A
symmetry-breaking mechanism: the only statement available is that the stabilizer of the background under global
$SU(2)$ is its centralizer in $SU(2)$, the center $\{\pm I\}$; that is a property of a fixed background, not a
mechanism. (v) The "non-abelian Bianchi identity" of Section 2.3 of the monograph: on any 2-complex the based,
ordered product of boundary-plaquette holonomies of a 3-cell is the identity for every connection; this is a
combinatorial tautology, and the monograph's unordered product is not well defined for a non-abelian group.

**Assumptions that would have to be added** to obtain gauge dynamics: dynamical link variables in a Lie group
containing the background; a Wilson or heat-kernel action with coupling $\beta$; a matter action; and a continuum
limit, which Section 2.2 shows is unavailable for the $Q_8$ data themselves.

### 5.3 Claim that is false as a theorem: the 4D $\mathrm{Spin}(4)$ obstruction

`paper/main.tex` Theorem `thm:open_problem_9_1` and the pure-math deliverable Section 4 assert that
$\operatorname{rank}_{\mathbb Q}\mathcal M_{\mathrm{obs}}=6$ proves no pure-rotational frame assignment on $C_4$ closes
under inflation. The matrix is built in `certify_4d_c4_obstruction_rank_exact` as follows: for `idx` in $0..63$, pair
boundary panel `idx` with boundary panel `(idx*7+3) mod 64`, take the wedge of their normals, and where that wedge
is zero insert $\pm1$ according to the parity of `idx//6`. No frame assignment, permutation, or inflation rule
appears. Replacing the magic pair $(7,3)$ by $(5,1)$, $(11,2)$, $(13,9)$ or $(1,0)$ gives matrices of rank 6 with
Gram determinants $25\,941\,348$, $25\,709\,716$, $23\,357\,132$, $1\,464\,100$: an almost arbitrary
$64\times6$ integer matrix has full column rank. `analyze_kamenetsky_open_problem_9_1` returns
`"algebraic_obstruction_verified": True` as a literal. The Lean theorems certify the arithmetic of the stored
matrix and nothing more. Kamenetsky's Open Problem 9.1 is not resolved by this repository.

### 5.4 Claim that is an artifact: the soldered-Laplacian "mass gap"

`src/chair44/solder_laplacian.py` assembles, per shared-panel pair $(u,v)$ with weight $w$ and normal projector $N$,
diagonal blocks $wI+M_u^\top NM_u$ and off-diagonal block $-(wI+M_u^\top NM_v)$. The $wI$ coupling is transported
with the identity, the $N$ coupling with the coboundary $M_u^\top M_v$. These are two inequivalent gauges in one
operator; the pure-gauge sections $x_a=M_a^\top\mathbf y$ that annihilate the second term do not annihilate the
first, and vice versa, so the kernel is empty for a trivial reason. Transporting both couplings with the same
connection, $-M_u^\top(wI+N)M_v$, gives trace 384, rank 21 and a three-dimensional kernel spanned by
$x_a=M_a^\top\mathbf y$ (verified to machine precision). The "lifting of translational zero modes" is not a
property of the geometry. Additionally the stored $24\times24$ matrix has $\lambda_{\min}=2.908906$; the published
value $2.6687265$ does not correspond to any matrix in the repository.

## 6. Physical verification

### 6.1 Established observables

None are recovered. No mass, coupling, mixing angle or cross-section is derived anywhere from the $Q_8$
