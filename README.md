## Unification-programme preprint and working record

Two folders hold a separate line of work on the same Chair44 object: a Q₈-type group-valued connection on the panel complex of the 3D chair substitution tiling, and the bottom ("floor") of its covariant Laplacian.

- [paper/toe-preprint-v0.6/](paper/toe-preprint-v0.6/): frozen draft v0.6 of the programme preprint, with LaTeX source, the 35-page PDF, the claim ledger (`claims.csv`, generated by `build_claims.py` from `claims_data.py`), the bibliography and changelogs v0.1–v0.6. It is an **unsubmitted, unrefereed draft** and still contains open `[TODO]` placeholders. See `RELEASE_NOTE.md` there for hashes and build steps. Figures 1–5 are in [paper/figures/proof-brackets/figs_v0.2/](paper/figures/proof-brackets/figs_v0.2/).
- [toe/](toe/): the research record behind it, with frozen preregistrations, scored results (failures included), certificate re-derivations, Lean 4 scratch proofs with kernel re-run logs, dated amendments, and a Regge/CDT feasibility scoping under `toe/gravity/`.

**Status.** "ToE" is an internal project name for a unification programme. It is not a theory of everything, and it makes no physical claim. The target is a condition, not an award: every row of the physics scorecard proved or certified, each at its stated scope, is necessary before the larger name could be used, and it would not by itself be enough. Until then it stays a unification programme. Each open row is listed in [toe/OPEN_ATTACKS.md](toe/OPEN_ATTACKS.md) with a next attempt and a proposed pass/fail test, and attempts on them are invited. Every statement carries one of the labels [P] proved, [C] certified or computed (scope stated), [E] numerical or simulated evidence (real measurements are marked "(measured)"), [X] conjecture or open, [A] analogy only, or [F] falsified. The labels mean exactly what they say. A claim is never printed stronger than its source, and [A] stays analogy. The material was produced with AI assistance; machine-checked items are identified as such, hand proofs are marked unverified, and the rest is labelled as evidence or conjecture. SHA-256 hashes for the frozen preprint files are in [paper/toe-preprint-v0.6/RELEASE_NOTE.md](paper/toe-preprint-v0.6/RELEASE_NOTE.md).
